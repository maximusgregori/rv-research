RV Trade-Delta Benchmark

Industry dataset of lowest published asking prices versus trade value, one row per manufacturer → model → floor plan.

Methodology

  • Listing sources: rvtrader.com and rvt.com
  • Industry research filters: New, year 2026+, length under 40 ft
  • Trade value = J.D. Power Low Retail × 0.9
  • Delta = lowest ask − trade
  • Default sort: smallest delta first. Click a column header to change sort.
  • One row per manufacturer → model → floor plan
