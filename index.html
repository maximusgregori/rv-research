Methodology

  • Listing sources: rvtrader.com and rvt.com
  • Filters: New, year 2026+, length under 40 ft
  • Trade value = J.D. Power Low Retail × 0.9 (Dale’s rule)
  • Delta = lowest ask − trade
  • Sort target eventually: smallest delta first
  • One row per manufacturer → model → floor plan when scaled; this page is the pilot
